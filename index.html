From a curious kid to AI for a city.

I'm Madhura — I build AI systems for people who don't work in tech, and who have to trust the answer. Here's how I got here.

The journey

🇮🇳

01 · Where it starts

A curious kid in India

School · India

School was where the questions started — and where a computer first felt less like a machine and more like a place to build things. My first language was Java; loops, logic, the small thrill of making something run.

🎓

02 · The first real thing

Teaching a machine to find justice

Computer Engineering · University of Mumbai

In college I built an ML model to surface relevant judicial cases — pulling the most recent, most relevant precedent from a pile of legal text instead of leaving someone to search by hand. The first time I saw the shape of what I'd keep doing.

🛠️

03 · Learning to ship

Building the plumbing

Data Engineer · Accenture, Mumbai · 2018–2021

My first job — 50+ production pipelines moving data across 20+ systems, with reconciliation and validation written in so failures surfaced instead of hiding. Where I learned that the data being right is the whole job.

✈️

04 · The plan changed

I wanted to be a founder. I moved instead.

Moved to the USA · August 2021

The plan was an MBA — I always wanted to build something of my own. Life had other timing. Instead of business school, I moved to the US for a Master's in Computer Science at Syracuse University. The founder instinct didn't leave; it found a different door.

📚

05 · Building for others

Custom learning, for local schools

Intern · WCNY · December 2022

I helped build custom learning courses for local schools — technology in service of teachers and kids. It cemented the thread through everything: the person on the other end usually isn't technical, and the work is only good if it works for them.

🏛️

06 · Where it landed

AI for a city

Data Analyst · City of Rochester, New York · 2023–2026

I built AI and data systems inside government — a four-node agent on AWS Bedrock, retrieval that kept policy answers grounded in real documents, and the judgment to escalate to a human when the machine shouldn't decide alone. I also spoke at Flower City AI on deploying AI people can trust.

🍁

07 · What's next

New country, open to what's next

Cambridge, Ontario · Canada · Open to work

My time with the City has wrapped, and I've moved to Canada as a permanent resident. I'm looking for a team building AI that real people have to trust — agents, RAG, data systems, the unglamorous parts done right. Let's talk.
